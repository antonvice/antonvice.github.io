The post serves a 704 x 848 H.264 video containing 30 animated banners
in a three-column, ten-row grid. Each GIF is a separate crop of that video,
preserving the full 35.9-second recording at 10 fps, resized to 190 x 68.
Matching PNGs contain the first GIF frame for reduced-motion display.

Crop: 228 x 82; x = 12, 243, 472; y = 10 + (84 * row), with rows 0–9.
GIF encoding: 64-color palette, no dithering, infinite loop.

Each side of the profile samples up to 20 of the 30 original GIFs without
replacement. Both sides shuffle independently every five seconds. The
available height determines how many banners fit above the projects section.
Reduced-motion mode displays the matching PNGs and keeps their order still.

To rebuild the older rotation-*.gif composites from the repository root:
node scripts/build-ad-rotations.mjs

All files are same-origin assets; the page does not load media from Telegram.
